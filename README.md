# Meteorological-Factors-and-HFMD
This repository contains an analysis of the association between meteorological factors and hand, foot, and mouth disease (HFMD), as well as a predictive model, including relevant code files, data, computational results, and model files.

该代码仓库包含气象因素与手足口病（HFMD）的关联分析研究及预测模型，涵盖相关代码文件、数据资料、计算结果与模型文件。
# 背景 Background
Hand, foot, and mouth disease (HFMD) is a group of acute, febrile, exanthematous infectious diseases caused by enteroviruses (EVs), primarily affecting children under the age of 5. The pathogens responsible for HFMD include enterovirus 71, coxsackievirus A4 (CA4),CA6, CA10, and CA16, with CVA16 and EV71 being the most common causes of HFMD.Most children with HFMD present with typical clinical features such as maculopapular rashes, vesicles, and/or ulcers in the mouth, on the hands and feet, and on the buttocks; if the condition is not treated promptly and progresses further, patients may develop neurological, respiratory, and circulatory dysfunction, leading to rapid clinical deterioration; critical complications include neurogenic pulmonary edema (NPE), aseptic meningitis, brainstem encephalitis, myocarditis, and cardiopulmonary failure. At the same time, HFMD is prevalent to varying degrees worldwide, and is particularly common in Asia. The incidence of HFMD in XX Province is higher than the national average, and the incidence in XX City is among the highest in the province. Previous studies suggest that meteorological factors may influence the occurrence and spread of HFMD; therefore, when developing predictive models for HFMD, it is necessary to account for the complex nonlinear relationship between meteorological factors and HFMD.

手足口病（HFMD）是一组由肠道病毒（EVs）引起的急性发热性皮疹传染病，主要影响5岁以下儿童。导致手足口病的病原体包括肠道病毒71型、柯萨奇病毒A4型（CA4）、CA6型、CA10型和CA16型，其中柯萨奇病毒A16型和肠道病毒71型是手足口病最常见的病因。大多数手足口病患儿表现为典型的临床特征，如口部、手足及臀部出现斑丘疹、水疱和/或溃疡；若未及时治疗且病情进一步发展，患者可能出现神经系统、呼吸系统和循环系统功能障碍，导致临床状况迅速恶化； 危重并发症包括神经源性肺水肿（NPE）、无菌性脑膜炎、脑干脑炎、心肌炎以及心肺功能衰竭。同时，手足口病在全球范围内不同程度地流行，在亚洲地区尤为常见。XX省的手足口病发病率高于全国平均水平，而XX市的发病率在全省范围内名列前茅。既往研究表明，气象因素可能影响手足口病的发生和传播；因此，在构建手足口病预测模型时，有必要考虑气象因素与手足口病之间复杂的非线性关系。
# Objective
* Describe the spatiotemporal epidemiological patterns of HFMD;
* To investigate the association between meteorological conditions and the risk of hand, foot, and mouth disease (HFMD) and to identify meteorological factors that significantly influence the incidence of HFMD;
* By combining HFMD incidence data with identified meteorological factors, we established a prediction model for hand, foot, and mouth disease.

* 通过将手足口病发病率数据与已确定的气象因素相结合，我们建立了一个手足口病预测模型。
# The Significance of This Work
An analysis of the temporal, geographical, and demographic distribution of hand, foot, and mouth disease (HFMD) in XX city from 2018 to 2022 provides valuable insights into the overall situation of HFMD in the city. Investigating the association between HFMD and meteorological factors, as well as the strength of this association, and incorporating meteorological factors into a predictive framework to construct an HFMD prediction model will help elucidate the impact of meteorological factors on HFMD and identify trends in its development, thereby providing data-driven support for HFMD prevention and control.

对2018年至2022年XX市手足口病（HFMD）的时空及人口分布情况进行分析，有助于深入了解该市手足口病的总体状况。通过探究手足口病与气象因素之间的关联及其关联强度，并将气象因素纳入预测框架以构建手足口病预测模型，有助于阐明气象因素对手足口病的影响并识别其发展趋势，从而为手足口病的防控提供数据驱动的支持。
# Materials and Methods
* Conduct a descriptive analysis of the temporal, spatial, and population distribution of hand, foot, and mouth disease (HFMD) in XX City from 2018 to 2022. The temporal analysis will focus on short-term fluctuations, seasonality, periodicity, and long-term trends in HFMD. Investigate the association between meteorological factors and HFMD, and incorporate these factors into a predictive framework to construct a forecasting model based on historical HFMD data and meteorological factors.
* Based on the administrative divisions of XX City, the age distribution of HFMD cases, and occupational characteristics, the incidence rates of HFMD were calculated by gender, age, region, and population group. Statistical methods (chi-square test, Kruskal-Wallis H test, Dunn’s test, etc.) were then used to analyze differences in HFMD incidence rates across different genders, age groups, regions, and occupations. The temporal distribution of HFMD was analyzed using STL decomposition, ACF, PACF, and mathematical and statistical methods. Spearman’s correlation coefficient and the Poisson GAM model were used to analyze the association between meteorological factors and HFMD and to investigate the impact of changes in meteorological factors on HFMD. Finally, ARIMA, SARIMA, and ARIMAX models were combined with the machine learning method XGBoost to build predictive models for HFMD using meteorological factors and historical HFMD data as inputs.
---
* 对2018年至2022年XX市手足口病（HFMD）的时空分布及人群分布情况进行描述性分析。时间分析将重点关注手足口病的短期波动、季节性、周期性及长期趋势。探究气象因素与手足口病之间的关联，并将这些因素纳入预测框架，基于历史手足口病数据和气象因素构建预测模型。
* 基于XX市的行政区划、手足口病病例的年龄分布及职业特征，按性别、年龄、地区和人口群体计算了手足口病的发病率。随后采用统计学方法（卡方检验、克鲁斯卡尔-瓦利斯H检验、邓恩检验等）分析了不同性别、年龄组、地区和职业间手足口病发病率的差异。利用STL分解、ACF、PACF以及数学和统计方法，对手足口病的时空分布进行了分析。采用斯皮尔曼相关系数和泊松GAM模型，分析了气象因素与手足口病之间的关联，并探讨了气象因素变化对手足口病的影响。最后，将ARIMA、SARIMA和ARIMAX模型与机器学习方法XGBoost相结合，以气象因素和历史手足口病数据作为输入，构建了手足口病预测模型。
